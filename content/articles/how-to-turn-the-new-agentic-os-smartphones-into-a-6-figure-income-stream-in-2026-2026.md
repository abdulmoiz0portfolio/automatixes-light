<p>Yes. The Agentic Marketplace allows you to embed optional subscriptions, in‑app purchases, or ad‑based revenue even for free‑download apps. The key is to provide clear value upgrades that leverage the NPU, such as premium AI filters or advanced analytics.</p>

<h3>How does revenue sharing work with the Agentic Marketplace?</h3>
<p>The standard split is 85% to the developer and 15% to the platform. For high‑volume enterprise deals, you can negotiate a custom split, but the base rates apply to consumer subscriptions and pay‑per‑use APIs.</p>

<h3>Is there a risk of my on‑device model being reverse‑engineered?</h3>
<p>Agentic OS encrypts <code>.onx</code> binaries with device‑specific keys and runs them inside a Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). While no system is 100% tamper‑proof, the barrier is high enough that most developers consider it secure for commercial use.</p>

<h3>What compliance considerations should I keep in mind?</h3>
<p>Because data never leaves the device, you automatically meet many privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA). However, if you export federated insights, you must ensure differential privacy parameters meet local standards. The Federated Edge Console provides built‑in compliance reports to simplify audits.</p>
